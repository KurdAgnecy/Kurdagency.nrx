Privacy Policy

Kurd Agency was founded in 2018. Owner: Aryan Ali Abdulla. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account (only for games and invites): username, full name, phone number, email, password (stored hashed; we cannot read it), optional profile photo, points and invite code.
  • Sponsorship requests: page name, post link, business type, cities or audience, gender and age, duration, payment method, amount and invite code.
  • Technical: a random visitor ID in your browser to count visits, and the TikTok Pixel for analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Ad data: if you order Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads from us, we may retrieve your ad results (reach, clicks, messages) from the Meta Marketing API and show them only to you.

Why we use it

To run your sponsorship, contact you on WhatsApp, operate your account, points and invites, and keep statistics.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with services we need: Vercel (hosting), Upstash (database), Meta/TikTok/Snapchat (to run the ads you ordered) and WhatsApp.

Deletion

You can request deletion of your data: /delete.html.
